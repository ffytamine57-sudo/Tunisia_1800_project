# Tunisia 1800 AI Video Project - Scene 1

## Project Overview
* **Setting:** Tunis, Tunisia (circa 1800, late Husainid era).
* **Main Character (Ahmed):** 35-year-old Tunisian water carrier, wearing a red Chechia (fez), indigo blue embroidered vest (fermla), cream tunic, wide beige sarouel trousers, carrying heavy earthenware pots (jraf) with a wooden yoke.
* **Supporting Character (Laila):** Wife, wearing an ivory white Sefsari.

## Master Cinematic Prompt (for Image & Video AI)
Cinematic wide 8k movie scene set in Tunis, Tunisia circa 1800. A poignant morning farewell between a traditional North African couple in a narrow cobblestone alleyway of the historic Medina. 

CHARACTER 1: "Ahmed", a hardworking 35-year-old Tunisian water carrier with weathered features and determined expression. He wears a dark indigo blue embroidered vest (fermla), a cream cotton tunic, wide beige sarouel trousers, and a deep red traditional Chechia (fez) on his head. Balanced across his strong shoulders is a heavy rustic wooden yoke (harness) with thick ropes suspending two large wet earthenware clay water pots (jraf) dripping with fresh water droplets.

CHARACTER 2: His wife "Laila", a graceful Tunisian woman in her late 20s wearing a traditional ivory white silk Sefsari wrapped elegantly around her, subtly revealing her expressive warm eyes and modest posture. She stands near the wooden door of an authentic whitewashed house with blue studded trim, bidding him farewell with care.

ENVIRONMENT & ATMOSPHERE: Early morning golden hour light filter through stone arches, casting dramatic shadows on the historic cobblestone ground.
